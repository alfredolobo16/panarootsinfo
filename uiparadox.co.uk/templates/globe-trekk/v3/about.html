Why PanaRoots exists

About

Panama is known for its canal, beaches and landscapes. PanaRoots begins with the traditions, flavors and cultures that live behind those places.

The idea

Without roots, there is no future.

Many visitors reach Panama without an easy way to discover the country's cultural depth. PanaRoots was created as a tourism platform that helps international travelers find authentic Panamanian experiences grounded in culture, traditions, gastronomy and local communities.

The vision goes beyond selling a tour. It is about creating memorable experiences, supporting local tourism and inviting visitors to return with a deeper understanding of Panama.

How the platform helps

From curiosity to a better-planned visit.

PanaRoots organizes information by destination, experience and date, so visitors can move through the site with a clear purpose.

Discover

Use destination context, photographs and recommendations to understand what makes each region distinct.

Connect

Find experience concepts linked to culture, food, crafts, festivals and the people who keep those traditions alive.

Plan

Match travel dates with the cultural calendar, then confirm the practical details before reserving.

What matters

Culture is carried by people.

PanaRoots is designed around respect, accurate information and meaningful local participation.

Local partnership

Experiences should create room for local guides, makers, cooks and cultural practitioners to participate on clear terms.

Cultural context

Visitors should know the meaning behind an object, dish, dance or place, not only its name.

Honest details

Prices, schedules and services are published only when they are confirmed for a real experience.

Responsible growth

The platform aims to support tourism that values community knowledge and encourages visitors to keep discovering Panama.
