Panama ProvinceHistory, museums and food

Panama City and the Canal

Walk the plazas of Casco Antiguo, trace the city's earlier chapter at Panama Viejo and learn how the canal shaped Panama's global role. The capital also offers museums, craft markets and a broad gastronomic scene.

Los Santos and HerreraFolklore and artisan heritage

Azuero Peninsula

Often associated with Panama's folkloric heart, Azuero brings together polleras, tamborito, mejorana, artisan work and celebrations such as the Thousand Polleras Parade and Corpus Christi.

ChiriquiCoffee, farms and highlands

Boquete

A cool mountain valley known for coffee farms, seasonal produce and forest landscapes. It is a strong setting for food stories, producer visits and nature-led cultural routes.

Bocas del ToroCaribbean culture and cacao

Bocas del Toro

Beyond the archipelago's beaches, Bocas carries Afro-Caribbean rhythms, Ngabe-Bugle connections, cacao traditions and communities shaped by water, forest and island life.

Colon ProvinceCongo heritage and coastal history

Portobelo and the Caribbean Coast

Colonial history, the Black Christ devotion and the living Congo tradition make this coast central to understanding Panama's Afro-descendant heritage.
