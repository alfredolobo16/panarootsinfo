Made by hand

Craft Workshops

PanaRoots wants visitors to see craftsmanship as living knowledge, not simply a souvenir. Workshops can introduce the process, history and meaning behind forms such as ceramics, tembleques and the sombrero pintao.

The platform also creates space for local artisans to present their work and share the stories carried by each piece.

  • Tembleques
  • Sombrero pintao

Shared celebration

Festivities and Traditions

Panama's festive calendar brings costume, music, dance, faith and community memory into public space. PanaRoots helps visitors understand what they are seeing and plan around confirmed dates.

Key references in the original concept include Carnival, the Thousand Polleras Parade, Corpus Christi and regional parades.

  • Carnival
  • Thousand Polleras
  • Corpus Christi
  • Parades

Stories at the table

Gastronomic Tours

Panamanian cuisine reflects migration, geography and cultural exchange. A PanaRoots food experience should connect flavors with the people, places and histories behind them.

The concept highlights familiar dishes such as hojaldre, tamales and patacones, while leaving the final menu to the confirmed region and host.

  • Hojaldre
  • Tamales
  • Patacones
  • Regional ingredients

Past and present

Cultural Tours

Cultural routes can connect architecture, museums, oral history, crafts and local communities. The aim is not to collect landmarks, but to understand how Panama's many identities meet.

Each future route should name its guide, access conditions and relationship with the people or institutions whose stories it presents.
