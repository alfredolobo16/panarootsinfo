Discover the Panama behind the postcard

Travel through culture, not around it.

PanaRoots is a tourism platform created to connect international visitors with authentic Panamanian experiences centered on culture, traditions, gastronomy and local communities.

It brings together practical recommendations, cultural context, experience ideas and space for local makers, helping each trip feel more personal and better connected to the people who give Panama its character.

  • Living heritage
  • Local craftsmanship
  • Traditional flavors
  • Community connection
