PLACES THAT ROOT YOU IN PANAMA

PANAMA CANAL

The canal is Panama's living bridge between oceans, history, and global movement. At Miraflores, visitors can watch ships rise through the locks while guides connect the engineering marvel with the communities that grew around it. It is a powerful first stop for understanding the country's scale, identity, and strategic heart.

CONTADORA ISLAND

Located in the Las Perlas archipelago, Contadora is a calm Pacific retreat with clear water, white sand, and a pearl-trade past. Today it is ideal for snorkeling, boat days, seasonal whale watching, and quiet luxury close to Panama City. It adds a softer island rhythm to the PanaRoots experience.

PANAMA VIEJO

Panama Viejo preserves the ruins of the first European city on the Pacific coast of the Americas, founded in 1519. Walking through its stone streets, towers, and museum spaces reveals the roots of the capital before modern Panama rose beside it. It is a must for travelers who want context, not just photos.
