BEST TRAVEL PLACES

PANAMA CANAL

They are impressive feats of modern engineering that act as hydraulic lifts to move ships between two oceans. They raise vessels to the level of Gatun Lake and then grardually lower them toward their final destination port. Complexes such as the Miraflores Locks offer visitor centers where one can witness this incredible process up close. They represent the heart of global maritime trade and the country's history of technical innovation.

CONTADORA ISLAND

Located in the Las Perlas archipelago, it is a tropical paradise surrounded by crystal-clear waters and white sands. During the colonial era, it served as a counting point for pearls harvested in the gulf—hence its distinctive name. Today, it offers an exclusive retreat ideal for humpback whale watching, snorkeling, and relaxation. Thanks to its abundant marine life, it stands out as one of the premier tourist gems of the Panamanian Pacific.

PANAMA VIEJO

This is the archaeological site where the first Spanish city on the Pacific coast of the Americas was founded in 1519. Its impressive historical ruins preserve the memory of the former capital prior to its destruction by Henry Morgan. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, it allows visitors to explore colonial history. Its famous cathedral tower offers a stunning panoramic view of both modern and ancient architecture.
